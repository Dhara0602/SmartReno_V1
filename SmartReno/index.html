Capabilities

Everything you need to renovate

Professional-grade tools, free to use, no account required.

🧠

AI Room Analysis

Claude Vision API analyzes your room photo to detect lighting, proportions, and existing style — then generates a tailored colour palette, flooring choices, and furniture list.

🎮

3D Visualization

Place AI-suggested furniture into a real-time Three.js 3D room. Rotate, scale, and reposition items with mouse or touch controls — see the space before you spend a penny.

📱

Mobile AR Overlay

On mobile, your live camera feed becomes the 3D scene background — giving a genuine pseudo-AR preview of furniture in your actual room environment.

💷

Budget Planning

Automatically calculates materials cost, 20% labour, and 10% contingency. Shows whether you're on budget, moderate, or over — with a clear colour-coded status badge.

📄

PDF Export

Download a branded, multi-page PDF report with your room photo, AI recommendations, colour palette swatches, itemized cost breakdown, and project summary — ready to share with contractors.

⚡

Instant Results

No waiting, no subscriptions, no setup. Upload your photo, pick your style, and get a full design recommendation in under 30 seconds. Your data is never stored.
